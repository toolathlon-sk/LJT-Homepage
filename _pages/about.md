I am a first-year PhD candidate at [HKUST NLP Group][1], advised by [Professor Junxian He][2]. I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)][3] in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring how large language models can enhance their reasoning capabilities through reinforcement learning approaches.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Investigating the causes and mitigation strategies for hallucinations in vision-language systems.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Understanding the truthfulness of language models and developing methods to make them more interpretable.
